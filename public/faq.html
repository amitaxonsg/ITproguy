Answers & guidance

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about what Axel can do today, how guidance time works, and how we handle accounts, safety and billing.

Current service status
Axel provides text guidance. Browser actions and checkout are currently unavailable, so no website changes or payments are made.
What can Axel do right now?

Describe an IT or software question and Axel will provide step-by-step text guidance. Axel does not currently control a browser, connect to third-party accounts, edit files or make changes on your behalf. Please review each step before taking it.

Can Axel update my website or complete a task for me?

Not yet. Browser actions and connected-service changes are disabled. Axel can explain how you can make a change yourself, but it cannot sign in to your website, publish content, edit your files or confirm a change was completed.

How do I sign in?

Use an email sign-in code, Google or Microsoft when each option is available on the sign-in screen. Your profile menu shows the account currently signed in. If a provider account is already linked elsewhere, contact support for help recovering the right account.

How is guidance time used?

Successful guidance is metered by elapsed processing time and is deducted from trial time first, then from available paid time. Failed or review-paused requests are not charged. There is no product-level daily task quota. Model/provider technical limits, temporary rate limits and fair-use protections can still apply. If the service asks you to wait, try again after 15 minutes.

Will I be charged or renewed automatically?

No. Checkout is disabled and no payment is collected. Recurring renewals, card storage and paid subscriptions are not active.

Where are my invoices and purchase history?

No checkout is active, so ITProGuy cannot issue an invoice or receipt for a new purchase. Your account activity page shows guidance and sign-in activity. If you have a legacy payment, contact support with its date and reference so we can review it.

How do cancellations and refunds work?

There is no active subscription to cancel and no payment flow to request a refund through. If you believe you were charged for ITProGuy, contact rico.m@axon.com.sg with the transaction reference. We will investigate through the payment provider records.

Can I upload a profile photo?

Yes. In your profile settings you can upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 8 MB. It is resized to a private 256-pixel JPEG and is not sent to Axel. It is not publicly visible and is not automatically content-moderated. Do not upload sexual, abusive or unlawful content.

How do you review abuse and harmful content?

Requests that appear unsafe may be refused or paused for review. We prohibit sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, non-consensual intimate imagery, harassment, hate, malware, fraud and unauthorized access. You can submit a manual account review request or abuse report from Profile → Account activity; it enters the restricted administrator queue. Automated abuse scoring, a separate appeal portal and profile-photo moderation are not available.

How can I get help?

Open the Help page for safe troubleshooting steps and support options, or email rico.m@axon.com.sg.
