Account and sign-in

Use the email-code, Google or Microsoft options shown on the sign-in page. Check that you are using the same provider account you linked previously. If sign-in returns you to the welcome screen, retry once and then send support the approximate time and provider used. Do not email a verification code.

Guidance time and service interruptions

Successful guidance is metered against trial time first and then unexpired paid balance. Failed or review-paused requests are not charged. Temporary provider limits can delay responses. If Axel asks you to wait, please wait 15 minutes before retrying. Checkout is disabled; support cannot add paid time through this page.

Safety and account review

Unsafe or abusive requests may be refused. To ask us to review a decision or report abuse, email rico.m@axon.com.sg. Include the account email and a brief description; do not attach sensitive identity documents or explicit images. Open Profile → Account activity to send a manual account review request or abuse report to the restricted administrator queue. Automated detection, a separate appeals portal, a response-time commitment and photo moderation are not available.

Contact Axon support

Email rico.m@axon.com.sg and include your ITProGuy sign-in email, time of the problem (with timezone), browser/device, and a short description. Never send passwords, OTPs, API keys, payment card details or private files.

For a payment concern, include the date, currency and transaction reference only. No payment processor is currently connected to checkout.
