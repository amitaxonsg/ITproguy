Renewal and cancellation

Monthly renewal is not available. There is no active ITProGuy subscription to cancel and no payment method is stored by this site.

Invoices and tax

Because checkout is disabled, ITProGuy cannot create a new order, tax invoice or payment receipt today. If you believe you have a legacy ITProGuy charge, contact rico.m@axon.com.sg with the transaction date and provider reference.

Refunds and payment disputes

No new payment can be made here. For a suspected charge, email support for a transaction review. When checkout is introduced, the final refund, dispute, tax and consumer-rights terms will be published with the payment flow before purchase is enabled.

Fair use and model availability

There is no daily task count limit, but model/provider technical limits, safety checks and temporary rate limits remain in effect. Premium model availability may vary. If asked to wait, retry after 15 minutes. See the Fair Use Policy for details.

Important before launch

The price is a preview, not an offer to purchase. Business owner and legal review must confirm the final currency, applicable taxes, credit validity and consumer terms before payment collection begins.
