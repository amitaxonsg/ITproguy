Trust, privacy & service policies

Plain-language information about ITProGuy and Axel. The current release offers signed-in accounts and text guidance. It does not execute browser actions or accept payments.

Updated 31 October 2026 | Contact: rico.m@axon.com.sg

Current service status: Text guidance is available. Checkout, purchases, renewals, refunds, invoices and live website or browser actions are not enabled. Do not send payment details in chat or email.

Privacy notice

ITProGuy is provided by Axon 1Pro Solutions. We use personal information to create and secure your account, provide guidance, maintain usage records, prevent misuse and respond to support requests.

Information we handle

  • Account email, display name and sign-in provider information needed to authenticate you.
  • Your optional profile photo. It is resized in your browser, stored as a private JPEG in your signed-in account, and is not sent to Axel or shown publicly.
  • The text you send to Axel and its response while the request is processed. The task list shown in the current web app is held in the open browser session and clears when you reload. The service records account-level usage time, model, response status and request metadata for metering and service security.
  • Sign-in, session and technical records needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service, and support messages you send to Axon.

AI processing

When you ask for guidance, your request text is sent to Anthropic's commercial API to generate a response. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, payment-card data, private keys or sensitive personal or client data unless authorized and necessary. We do not send your profile photo to the model. Anthropic states that commercial API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default and are normally deleted within 30 days, subject to safety, legal or other agreed exceptions. See Anthropic retention details and training details.

Sign-in and service providers

Google and Microsoft handle their own sign-in process. ITProGuy uses the identity information needed to sign you in; it does not currently connect to your Google Drive or Microsoft 365 files. An email delivery provider sends one-time sign-in codes; a hosting provider runs the site and account service. Providers may process information in locations where their infrastructure operates. We apply contractual and other safeguards where required by applicable law.

Retention and your choices

Account, profile-photo, sign-in and usage records are kept while needed to provide and secure your account, resolve disputes, support billing records and meet legal duties. Information is deleted or de-identified when no longer reasonably needed, subject to legal holds and security requirements. The current service has no self-service export or account-deletion tool. To request access, correction, deletion or information about your data, email rico.m@axon.com.sg from your account email. We may verify your identity before acting.

Where applicable, individuals may have rights under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act and the Philippines' Data Privacy Act, including rights to information, access, correction and other rights provided by law. Rights and exceptions depend on the circumstances and jurisdiction. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and browser storage

ITProGuy uses a Secure, HttpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in and browser storage for interface preferences. Profile photos are private account data, not public profile images.

Terms of use

By using ITProGuy, you agree to use it lawfully and follow the Safety and Fair Use rules below. You must be at least 18 and have authority to act for any business or account you represent.

What Axel does today

Axel provides text-based IT guidance. It may explain steps, draft text or help diagnose an issue. It does not currently log in to external services, change websites, make purchases, send messages or execute browser actions. Review all guidance, verify facts and decide whether to act. AI responses can be incomplete or wrong; they are not a substitute for a qualified professional, incident response or backup.

Your responsibilities

  • Use only accounts, websites, devices and information you are authorized to manage.
  • Check instructions before applying them, especially DNS, email, production, billing, access-control or customer-data changes.
  • Keep credentials and verification codes out of chat. Do not ask Axel to bypass access controls or impersonate anyone.
  • Do not rely on the service for emergencies or time-critical security response. Contact your administrator or support provider.

Availability and content

We may update, suspend or limit service for maintenance, security, legal compliance or provider outages. You retain rights in material you submit and must have permission to submit it. You allow Axon and service providers needed to operate Axel to process it to provide, protect and support the service, subject to this Privacy Notice. You are responsible for your use of AI guidance and for approvals before acting for a customer or employer.

We do not exclude liability for fraud, deliberate misconduct or other liability that applicable law says cannot be excluded. If something goes wrong, contact us promptly.

Suspension and account review

We may pause a request or restrict an account for a credible safety, abuse, fraud or security concern. Where lawful and safe, we will notify the account holder and provide a way to contact support. A dedicated automated review dashboard is not currently available. Ask for manual review at rico.m@axon.com.sg. Do not create another account to evade a restriction.

Safety & acceptable use

ITProGuy is for legitimate IT support. Requests, photos and account activity must not be used to:

  • Create, request, store or share child sexual abuse material or sexual content involving minors. We may preserve and report information where required by law.
  • Create or distribute non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual deepfakes, pornography, sexual exploitation or sexual services.
  • Threaten, stalk, harass, extort, target or expose private information about a person.
  • Plan violence, facilitate self-harm, promote terrorism or evade law enforcement.
  • Steal credentials, access systems without permission, deploy malware, evade security controls, commit fraud or damage a service.
  • Upload illegal, abusive, infringing or deceptive material, or use another person's likeness without permission.
  • Disrupt ITProGuy, evade service limits or interfere with another account.

Profanity alone does not establish abuse; context matters. Safety systems or providers may refuse or pause a request. Serious or repeated violations can lead to temporary restrictions or suspension. If you think a restriction was mistaken, email support with your account email and event time. Do not send illegal imagery to support.

Profile photos are private to your account but must follow these rules. They are not published to other users or analyzed by an image-moderation model. Do not upload sexual, violent, hateful or abusive images.

You can submit an account-review request or abuse report from Profile → Account activity. Requests enter a restricted administrator queue for manual review. A reviewer may set an account to active, under review or suspended; under-review and suspended accounts cannot request new guidance. You can still sign in to view activity and ask for review. Reviews are not automated, and we do not promise a response time. The profile-photo feature has no automatic image moderation.

Fair use & model availability

Axel uses commercial AI models and service providers. This release has no published per-day task count or additional product-level token allowance. Each completed guidance request uses available account time. The selected model and provider still impose technical input and output maximums. Failed or safety-paused requests are not charged by current metering. Provider outages, safety controls, one-request-at-a-time protection and upstream capacity limits may prevent a response.

Send one request at a time and let it finish. If Axel or a provider displays a temporary limit message, stop retrying and follow the wait time shown. If it says to try again in 15 minutes, wait the full 15 minutes. Repeated retries may extend the interruption. Do not split a disallowed or oversized request into smaller requests to bypass a safety or service limit.

Fair use is not a promise of unlimited provider capacity or that every prompt will be accepted. We aim to explain safety pauses and provide a route to support. Guidance time is metered only while the request is processed; an idle login session is not billable work.

Security information

Current safeguards include HTTPS, a Secure HttpOnly session cookie, one-time email codes, Google or Microsoft sign-in, request-origin checks on account-changing actions, sign-in-code delivery limits, customer-session isolation and authenticated access to profile photos. Axel provides text guidance and can open public pages in a temporary isolated browser. It cannot sign in or change a connected account or website. Never share passwords or verification codes in chat.

These controls reduce risk but do not eliminate it and are not a security certification. The browser can open public web pages in a temporary isolated session, but cannot sign in to accounts or submit forms. The service does not store payment-card data or yet provide self-service account deletion. We do not claim independent penetration testing or a completed disaster-recovery exercise.

Report a suspected account compromise or security issue to rico.m@axon.com.sg. Include the account email, approximate time and short description. Do not include passwords, tokens, recovery codes or malware. For immediate risk to production, contact your administrator and rotate credentials through the provider.

Payments, credits & refunds

Checkout is disabled. ITProGuy does not currently accept payments, create credit purchases, renew subscriptions or issue in-product refunds. No payment is collected through the current site.

If you believe Axon or a third party charged you in error, contact rico.m@axon.com.sg with the transaction date and provider reference. Do not email full card details. Any future purchase flow must show its price, currency, credit validity, renewal terms and applicable refund rights before payment.
